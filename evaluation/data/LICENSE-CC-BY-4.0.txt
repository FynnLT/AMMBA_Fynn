CHANGES MADE IN THIS REPOSITORY
-------------------------------
The published workbook is stored unmodified at

    raw/EC_EV_dataset (fixed error).xlsx
    SHA-256 f96ef125d1c0f49cf3288b17598c166062570c7baa6c4d2d25ac545813531c3a

Derived data generated from it for the evaluation campaign are NOT part of the
original work. They are produced by this repository's own code and are:

  - a selection of 100 of the 250 players as the reference community;
  - per-player net positions per market slot (load minus PV), rather than the
    two separate series of the original;
  - an extension of the original single day (96 periods) to a week
    (672 slots) by a per-day multiplicative draw, with its own recorded seed;
  - market areas derived from the battery parameter sheet, with a charging
    rule that is a modelling assumption of this work and not part of the
    dataset.

Derived files live in `derived/` and are not committed. Any figure published
from them is a result of this work, not of the cited dataset, and is reported
as such.
