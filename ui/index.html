C Preferences & Energy-Type Multipliers

Allocation rules applied during clearing (Guide §6, Phase 2): (1) mutual preferred partners are matched first at the clearing price, (2) the residual pool is split pro-rata, (3) energy-type multipliers adjust the per-trade price ex-post.

Green: final = clearing × (1 + green_multiplier)  ·  Grey: final = clearing × (1 − min(grey_levy, levy_cap))  ·  green bonuses are funded by grey levy revenue; if revenue is insufficient the bonus is scaled down (dynamic subsidy scaling).
